# Hireling

Commission board for WoW: Forever (interface 16001). Not a Retail or Classic Era addon.

People who have the addon share one board per faction and ruleset. Offers are work someone will do. Bids are jobs someone will pay for. Ask opens a box on the other person's addon. They answer Yes, No, or Other, and the answer pops back. Nothing is written in chat. Gold stays in the trade window.

## Install

Exit the game. Copy the `Hireling` folder into `Interface/AddOns`. Start the game and enable Hireling on the character screen.

## Commands

- `/hireling` or `/hl` opens the window
- `/hireling sync` joins the channel and sends your listings
- `/hireling sample` shows local sample cards that are not sent to anyone
- `/hireling clearsample` removes those samples
- `/hireling check` looks for setup problems
- `/hireling reset` centers the window
- `/hireling scale 1` sets the scale from 0.7 to 1.4
- `/hireling ruleset Normal` or `PvP`, or `Roleplaying`

Normal, PvP, and Roleplaying do not share a board. Alliance and Horde do not share one either.
